Beetle Glyph Engine

An icon library for the Beetle wallet, drawn through one parametric engine: every glyph is centrelines on a 24 grid, every radius and gap in stroke units. All of Lucide sits alongside the app's own glyphs and a set for every scenario in the app. Move the sliders and the whole library re-derives. Drag the points of any icon to edit it. Export SVG, a sprite, or an icon font.

EnginePolylines with per-vertex radius kinds, arcs, quadratic loops and SVG paths. Straight joins get true circular fillets. Quarter-circle corners in Lucide paths were recognised at import and follow the box corner slider.

    
Solid weightClosed shapes fill and inflate by S with round joins. Anything sitting inside a closed shape becomes a cut of S. Set any part by hand from the editor.

    
ExportsSVG keeps curves and the goo filter. Sprite and font flatten every primitive, offset it by S/2 with round joins and caps through Clipper, union and cut it. The font maps results to U+E000 upward in order.
