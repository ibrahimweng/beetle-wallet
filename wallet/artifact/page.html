The way in, from the loading screen to home

This is the app's own code, exported for the web. It opens as the app does: the welcome the first time, home after that. Open an account with any Nigerian number, or Sign in with the owner's number for the day the design is drawn around — pull the black card down there for the chat, try "Send 20k to Sarah" or "send 5k to $tobi", Send and Receive on the card, or the Send, Bills, Data, Receive and Loan chips over the chat's input, each a card in the chat; swipe left for Activities, and again for Settings. Nothing is texted and no register is asked, so these are the keys.

  1. Your number0812 345 6789
    Any Nigerian mobile number opens a fresh account. They start 070, 080, 081, 090 or 091.
  2. Six digits123456
    000000 has expired. Three that do not match send a fresh code on their own.
  3. Who you are1234 5678 900
    Comes back as Ibrahim Musa. Eleven digits with 0000 in them match nothing, and you see the other screen.
  4. Your faceTap Take it
    A moment's wait stands in for the camera here. On a phone it is the device's own face check. Or do it later.
  5. A passcode654321, twice
    The code backwards, which this build lets through; 123456 works too. Anything else that is a run, a repeated pair, one digit six times, the year of birth or one of the usual ones is refused, and it says which.
  6. Welcome back0803 000 0001 then 123456
    The demo account's number signs into it, history and all. Sign out first: it is in Settings, the gear on the bar at the foot of home, and it asks before it does.
  7. The chatSend 20k to Sarah
    A card comes up in the chat and pays from where it is: "Is this the person?" with Sarah's bank and number, the amount on the picker (tap the figure to type it), and Confirm, which goes to the passcode; the card says Send money, as the page does, whatever the bank. $tobi or $amaka is a Beetle account — free and there at once; a ten-digit number asks for the bank on the card, then checks the name. The card's button says what is still missing. Or "top up my light", "buy data", "borrow", "how much do I have". The camera at the end of the bar reads a sample slip here, and a real one on a phone.
  8. The three pagesSwipe left on home, or tap the bar
    Home, Activities and Settings sit side by side: a swipe moves them under the finger, the bar at the foot stays put. Under the black card are Savings, Loan, Card and Services; swipe the Services card itself for Airtime, Bills and Data, and tap the one showing. A settled line on Activities opens where it is: the line stays, the page goes soft under a white frost and the rest grows in under it, with Share receipt and Set it up; the ··· beside the title holds Ask Beetle about this and Report a problem, and a tap off it puts it all away. The bar stays under the open chat: Activities and Home again bring the chat back as it was, Home once more closes it. In the chat a receipt card opens where it is, a little larger; a swipe from the chat's left edge brings in the chats, inside the dark card, with New chat at the top. The plus opens More over a white veil.
  9. The chipsSend, Bills, Data, Receive, Save, Loan over the chat's input
    None leaves the chat. Bills is the light: the company, Prepaid or Postpaid, the meter number (try JED, Prepaid, 12345678901), then the amount and Pay. Recent at a card's top right grows the card into what was paid before. Receive is your number and your $tag to copy or share; Save puts money into a goal — pick the goal, the amount, Put away, the passcode; Loan is the amount, the days and what it costs, and Borrow. "save 10k for rent" typed is the Save card, filled; "my bills" is the month's bills; Services on home is the drawer, every tile a way in.
  10. Send moneySend on the card
    To is first, and takes all three: $tobi finds the Beetle account; a name brings the closest names with their banks; 0123456785 asks which bank, the likely ones first — pick GTBank and the name on the account is checked and shown. The bank is always said. Slide to send, and the passcode shows the whole of it — what they receive, the fee, what leaves Everyday — with Cancel under the pad.
  11. Amountsthe figure on any paying page
    Every amount is picked where it is asked for, never on a page of its own: drag the ruler under the figure — it steps ₦100 at a time up to ten thousand, ₦500 up to a hundred thousand, ₦1,000 above — tap a chip, or tap the figure and type it. It stops hard at what can be sent, the fee counted, and says All of it there. Borrow stops at the limit; Request money has no cap; the virtual card's Load is new, through the passcode to its receipt.
  12. Dollars and savingthe dollars chip on the card, the Savings card under it, Money health at the top of Activities
    Dollars is what is held and where it came from; Convert takes a figure and the passcode and lands on Converted. Send from there pays Sarah from the dollars, and the From row on any paying page opens Pay from. Savings opens the goal: Add money, Put away and the passcode is four taps from home, and Take out sits beside it. + New goal comes filled with Rent — Start saving is the third tap — and the goals sit as pills to switch between. The ··· edits, pauses or ends one; Money is tight in Standing instructions pauses them all. Money health is the score and the five habits behind it, with an offer to lift it.
  13. Being paidReceive on the card
    The sheet is your details: the account number and your $tag, Copy on each, and Share details for all of it at once. Ask someone opens a request; In dollars opens Dollars. Or type "ask musa for 20k for the rent balance", or show the camera the sample message: it reads Musa asking for 20k, and the request fills itself. Asking moves no money, so there is no passcode.
  14. When it goes wrongReport a problem under the ··· on a transfer's receipt
    They say it never arrived opens Your dispute: Beetle Dispute at work, with the day it is on. The demo's own dispute is on its third day, and the closed one is in the lab. On Send money, the note under a person the photo filled in opens Checking, how Beetle decided the figure. Send the whole balance to a number it has never paid — type 0123456785, pick GTBank, tap All of it — and Beetle will not do it, with three ways out; "send everything to 0123456789" in the chat gets the same answer. With the network off, the slide lands on You are offline, with the queue and USSD, and the chat says it will not send against a balance it cannot check.
  15. Finishing setting upFinish setting up on the ready screen, or the New account chip on a new account's card
    Where you live takes a street and an area; A photo of an ID takes a moment here and the camera on a phone; Where your money comes from is one tap; Everything is on lands the four things one after another. Spending limits then reads a million a day, and the chip is the dollars again. A new account's home, chat and Activities carry the first day's words: ask "What can you do?" there.
