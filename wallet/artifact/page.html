The way in, from the loading screen to home

This is the app's own code, exported for the web. It opens as the app does: the welcome the first time, home after that. Open an account with any Nigerian number, or Sign in with the owner's number for the day the design is drawn around — pull the black card down there for the chat, try "Send 20k to Sarah" or "ask musa for 20k", Send and Receive on the card, or Bills, Data and Services under the open chat; swipe left for Activities, and again for Settings. Nothing is texted and no register is asked, so these are the keys.

  1. Your number0812 345 6789
    Any Nigerian mobile number opens a fresh account. They start 070, 080, 081, 090 or 091.
  2. Six digits123456
    000000 has expired. Three that do not match send a fresh code on their own.
  3. Who you are1234 5678 900
    Comes back as Ibrahim Musa. Eleven digits with 0000 in them match nothing, and you see the other screen.
  4. Your faceTap Take it
    A moment's wait stands in for the camera here. On a phone it is the device's own face check. Or do it later.
  5. A passcode654321, twice
    The code backwards, which this build lets through; 123456 works too. Anything else that is a run, a repeated pair, one digit six times, the year of birth or one of the usual ones is refused, and it says which.
  6. Welcome back0906 911 3588 then 123456
    Your own number signs into your account, history and all. Sign out first: it is in Settings, the gear on the bar at the foot of home.
  7. The chatSend 20k to Sarah
    Or "top up my light", "buy data", "what about dollars", "how much do I have". It knows Sarah Adeyemi, Chidi Okafor, Musa Danjuma and John Doe, by name or account number. The camera at the end of the bar reads a sample slip here, and a real one on a phone.
  8. The three pagesSwipe left on home, or tap the bar
    Home, Activities and Settings sit side by side: a swipe moves them under the finger, the bar at the foot stays put. Under the black card are Savings, Loan, Card and Services; swipe the Services card itself for Airtime, Bills and Data, and tap the one showing. A settled line on Activities opens its receipt over the page — swipe right from the left edge, or Back, to put it away. In the chat a receipt card opens where it is, a little larger; the soft edge at the chat's left brings in the chats, with New chat at the top. The plus opens More over a white veil.
  9. Bills and dataBills, Data, Services under the open chat
    Bills is the month; a row opens Pay a bill, and the slide leads to the passcode and the receipt with the token. Data opens Buy data on Mum's line. Services is the drawer: every tile and row a way in, Loan among them, which lands as money in. The camera's gallery has a light bill and a message asking for data: the bill goes to What I found and Is this your meter?, the message to the chat that prices it.
  10. Dollars and savingthe dollars chip on the card, the Savings card under it, Money health at the top of Activities
    Dollars is what is held and where it came from; Convert takes a figure and the passcode and lands on Converted. Send from there pays Sarah from the dollars, and the From row on any paying page opens Pay from. Savings is the Holiday goal: Feed it more is the sheet of switches, Add money goes through the keypad; Money is tight in Standing instructions pauses it. Money health is the score and the five habits behind it, with an offer to lift it.
  11. Being paidReceive on the card
    A bank transfer opens the number to copy and the code to show; Ask someone opens a request. Or type "ask musa for 20k for the rent balance", or show the camera the sample message: it reads Musa asking for 20k, and the request fills itself. Asking moves no money, so there is no passcode.
  12. When it goes wrongSomething wrong with this? on a transfer's receipt
    They say it never arrived opens Your dispute: Beetle Dispute at work, with the day it is on. The demo's own dispute is on its third day, and the closed one is in the lab. On Send money, the note under a person the photo filled in opens Checking, how Beetle decided the figure. Send the whole balance to a number it has never paid — type 0123456789 and the balance — and Beetle will not do it, with three ways out; "send everything to 0123456789" in the chat gets the same answer. With the network off, the slide lands on You are offline, with the queue and USSD, and the chat says it will not send against a balance it cannot check.
  13. Finishing setting upFinish setting up on the ready screen, or the New account chip on a new account's card
    Where you live takes a street and an area; A photo of an ID takes a moment here and the camera on a phone; Where your money comes from is one tap; Everything is on lands the four things one after another. Spending limits then reads a million a day, and the chip is the dollars again. A new account's home, chat and Activities carry the first day's words: ask "What can you do?" there.
