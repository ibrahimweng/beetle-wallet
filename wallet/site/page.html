iPhone

  1. Install Expo Go from the App Store, or update it.
    It has to be the one for Expo SDK {{SDK}}: the latest in the store.
  2. Open the Camera and point it at the code. Tap Open in Expo Go when it shows.
  3. Beetle opens. To start again, shake the phone and tap Reload.

Android

  1. Install Expo Go from Google Play, or update it.
  2. Open Expo Go and tap Scan QR code, then point it at the code.
  3. Beetle opens. Shake the phone for Reload, or to go back to Expo Go's home.
